# Neutral cards, frames and arenas

Neutral cards show anonymous tournament fighters, so they need no portrait or sheet. Paste the Style bible from 00-start-here.md first. New cards are marked "(new)".

## Arena and frame references
- [The Fall of the Rebel Angels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fall_of_the_Rebel_Angels_(Bruegel)) (Pieter Bruegel the Elder): Teeming, layered chaos for The Pit
- [Catacombs of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Catacombs) (Paris): Walls of stacked bone for The Bone Gallery
- [Sedlec Ossuary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sedlec_Ossuary) (Kutná Hora): The Throne room
- [Melencolia I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melencolia_I) (Albrecht Dürer): Engraved ornament for card frames

## Card back, frames, foil, arenas and logo

### Card back

```
Design the back of a Marrow Rite trading card, vertical 5:7. Dark oxblood leather tooled with a symmetrical pattern of interlocking finger bones around a central sigil: a crown of teeth inside a ring. Tarnished gold leaf on the sigil, worn edges, subtle stitching around the border. Flat, front-on, evenly lit, perfectly symmetrical. No text.
```

### Rarity frames

```
Design five empty trading card frames for Marrow Rite, side by side, vertical 5:7 each, with the centre left flat grey for the art: 1) Basic: plain blackened iron. 2) Common: aged bone with hairline cracks. 3) Rare: cold steel with rivets and blue-grey enamel. 4) Epic: bruised violet lacquer over bone, filigree of vertebrae. 5) Legendary: gold leaf over carved bone, a crown of teeth at the top. Each frame has a round gem socket top-left for the cost, a name bar beside it, and a dark text box in the bottom third. Engraved ornament in the spirit of Dürer. No text.
```

### Bloodfoil overlay

```
A seamless holographic foil texture for premium cards: deep red and gold iridescent sheen with fine swirling patterns like blood in water, on a black background so it can be blended with Screen mode. Square, tileable, no text.
```

### Arena · The Pit

```
Wide establishing painting of The Pit, the first arena of Marrow Rite: a sunken sand-and-bone fighting floor at the bottom of a deep circular shaft, packed tiers of masked spectators climbing the walls, chains and cages hanging overhead, torchlight from above. Composition in the spirit of Bruegel's The Fall of the Rebel Angels and Doré's engravings. 16:9, no characters in the centre, no text.
```

### Arena · The Bone Gallery

```
Wide painting of The Bone Gallery, the second arena: a long vaulted hall whose walls are stacked skulls and long bones like the Catacombs of Paris, the trophies of every past Rite on display, candles in niches, a fighting floor of worn flagstones. 16:9, empty centre, no text.
```

### Arena · The Throne

```
Wide painting of the final arena, The Throne: a cathedral-sized chamber where every surface is built from bone like the Sedlec Ossuary, chandeliers of skulls, and at the far end a towering throne made of seated skeletons with one seat empty. Gold candlelight, silence, dust in the air. 16:9, no text.
```

### Logo mark

```
Logo emblem for "Marrow Rite": a crown made of human teeth set inside a cracked ring of bone, with a single drop of red at the base. Flat, iconic, works at small sizes, on a plain background. Include the words MARROW RITE in a sharp blackletter typeface beneath the emblem.
```

### Meter cost gem

```
A small game UI icon for a "Meter" cost: a faceted amber-and-orange gem shaped like a diamond, glowing from inside like an ember, with a thin tarnished-gold rim. Front-on, flat lighting, transparent or plain dark background, readable at 32 px. No text.
```
