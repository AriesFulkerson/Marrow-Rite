# Old Teeth, The Bog Mother

**Archetype:** Traps & venom control  
**HP:** 28  
**Passive, Patience:** Each unspent Energy at the end of your turn becomes 2 Guard. Sets hidden Traps.  
**Concept portrait:** old-teeth.png

> Ninety years in the drowned country. Her necklace is strung from the jaws of the men who came to hunt her.

**Look:** A stooped Black woman in her nineties, deep brown, deeply wrinkled skin, long grey-white locs tangled with strands of river weed, a milky clouded left eye, a delighted grin with only a few teeth. Layered, dripping moss-green rags and sackcloth; a necklace of beads and small bones with a human lower jawbone as its pendant. She grips a gnarled driftwood staff tipped with a crude spearhead and bound with cord and weed. Knee-deep in black swamp water among cypress, hanging moss, lily pads, mist and fireflies.

**Palette:** moss green, bog black, bone ivory, firefly yellow

## How to use this file
1. Open a new ChatGPT chat. Attach 2 or 3 of the style references (save an image from each link) and paste the **Style bible**.
2. Attach your concept portrait **old-teeth.png** (from marrow-rite-fighter-portraits.zip) plus Old Teeth's references, and paste the **Reference sheet** prompt. Save the sheet it makes.
3. For each card, attach the portrait **and** the saved sheet, then paste the card prompt. Crop to 5:7 and keep the bottom quarter dark for rules text.

## Old Teeth's references
- [The Garden of Earthly Delights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Garden_of_Earthly_Delights) (Hieronymus Bosch): Strange swamp creatures and traps
- [Witches' Sabbath (The Great He-Goat)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witches%27_Sabbath_(The_Great_He-Goat)) (Francisco Goya): Folk horror, crones, firelit darkness
- [Dulle Griet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dulle_Griet) (Pieter Bruegel the Elder): A fearless old woman striding through hell

## Style references (shared)
- [The Calling of Saint Matthew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Calling_of_Saint_Matthew) (Caravaggio): The lighting model: one hard warm source, deep black shadow
- [Black Paintings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Paintings) (Francisco Goya): Overall mood and muddy, desaturated palette
- [Saturn Devouring His Son](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturn_Devouring_His_Son) (Francisco Goya): How far the horror goes: dread over gore
- [The Triumph of Death](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Triumph_of_Death) (Pieter Bruegel the Elder): Crowds, arenas, the tournament as spectacle
- [Gustave Doré (Divine Comedy engravings)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustave_Dor%C3%A9) (Gustave Doré): Scale and depth for arenas and backgrounds
- [Judith Beheading Holofernes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judith_Beheading_Holofernes_(Caravaggio)) (Caravaggio): Violence at the moment of action, framed tight

## Step 1 · Style bible

```
You are illustrating "Marrow Rite", an original dark-fantasy collectible card game about a brutal underground fighting tournament held every hundred years in a citadel of bone by Vorhul, a king who refused to die.

Style for every image in this chat:
- Painterly oil-on-canvas finish, visible brush texture, grounded realistic anatomy.
- Heavy chiaroscuro: one hard, warm key light; deep near-black shadows.
- Desaturated earth palette (bone, ash, rust, leather, wet stone) with one saturated accent colour per image, taken from the fighter's palette.
- Mood: dread and violence that is implied, not shown. Wounds are suggested with shadow and dark stains; no exposed organs, no close-up gore.
- All characters are original. No likeness of any existing game, film, comic or real person.
- Never include text, letters, numbers, logos, borders, card frames or watermarks.
I have attached style references (Caravaggio, Goya, Bruegel). Match their lighting and mood, not their subjects. Reply "ready" and wait for my prompts.
```

Echoes are what a fighter's cards become after that fighter is knocked out. Make each one feel like an afterimage: desaturated, translucent, drifting, with the fighter's accent colour as the only saturated tone.
