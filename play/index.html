Marrow Rite

A collectible fighting-game card duel. Rip packs, build a three-fighter deck, and climb Vorhul's tournament. Every win pays out toward your next pack.

  1. IRip packs

    Five cards a pack, with a wild slot that can hit a Legendary. Duplicates become Bone Dust for crafting.

  2. IIBuild a team

    Pick three fighters and fill 30 cards from what you own. Basic strikes are always free.

  3. IIIFight the ladder

    Chain strikes, tag out, break combos. Win coins every match, more for a Last Rite.

  4. IVTake the throne

    Beat Vorhul to earn his cards and unlock him as a playable fighter.

    Art direction

    Prompts for ChatGPT

    Every prompt is built from the same card data the game uses, so names and effects always match. The references are public-domain artworks: open them, save an image or two, and attach them with the prompt.

    1. Paste the Style bible into a new ChatGPT chat first, with two or three of the style references attached.
    2. Attach the fighter's concept portrait and paste their reference sheet prompt. It turns your portrait into front, side and back views. Save the result.
    3. For each card, attach the portrait and the sheet, then paste the card prompt. Both together keep the face, build and outfit consistent.
    4. Crop to 5:7. Keep the bottom quarter dark; the rules text sits there.
    The gore stays implied (shadow, stains, aftermath), which keeps the prompts inside image-model content rules.

    How the Rite works

    Winning
    Knock out all three enemy fighters, or land a Last Rite on their final fighter.
    Turns
    Draw 2. Energy refills: 1 on your first turn, +1 each turn, up to 8. Your Guard from last turn fades. Benched fighters recover 2 HP a turn, up to 70% of their max.
    Chains
    Light (tier 1) into Medium (2) into Heavy (3). Each hit in a chain deals +1 for every hit before it. Dropping to a lower tier restarts the chain. Specials cancel into any chain without breaking it.
    Meter (max 3)
    Gain 1 when a chain reaches 3 hits and again at 5, when you take 6+ damage in one enemy turn, and when one of your fighters falls. Spend it on EX Specials (1), Finishers (1), Breakers (2) and Last Rites (3).
    Tagging
    Once per turn, tag a benched fighter in for 1 Energy. Tagging keeps your chain alive and cleanses Burn, Bleed, Venom and Noose from whoever leaves. A signature card of a benched fighter tags them in for +1 Energy.
    Blocks and Breakers
    Guard soaks damage until your next turn. An armed Breaker waits for the enemy chain to reach 3 hits, then stops it cold and takes 2 of their Energy.
    Finishers
    Need a chain of 2+ and 1 Meter. They hit like a Heavy and close the chain.
    Stronger as they weaken
    At 50% HP a fighter is Wounded (+1 damage per hit). At 25% they are Desperate (+2, and +1 Meter each turn). Once a teammate falls, the rest fight with Vengeance (+1 per hit). A fallen fighter's cards become Grudges: 1 Energy, 3 damage, +1 Meter.
    Final Round
    When each side has one fighter left, both heal to full, gain 2 Meter, and start every turn with 3 extra Guard. Only now can a Last Rite be played: 3 Meter, enemy at 6 HP or less.
    Playing a card
    Click a card in your hand to put it on the field as ready to play. Nothing happens yet, and your opponent can't see it. Press Play (or Enter) to resolve it, or Back to hand (or Esc) to change your mind. A played card stays on the field until your turn ends; its full math is in the fight record.
    Rounds and Blood Rounds
    A match lasts at most a set number of rounds (each player takes one turn per round), shown above the arena. In the last few rounds, Blood Rounds begin: at the start of each turn your active fighter takes unblockable damage that grows every round. In the last round the crowd storms the pit: at the start of your turn, every fighter on your team takes heavy damage. If both teams still have fighters standing when it ends, the team with the most total HP left wins.
    Fighter mechanics
    Heat (Ashkar) stays between turns, max 5, and adds to cards marked Heat. Bleed stacks up to 6. Retaliate: hits that land on Brannoch's Guard take damage back. Hunger (Wen): +1 per card you discard, max 6; her Specials deal +1 per 2 Hunger. Traps (Old Teeth) are hidden until the enemy springs them; 2 at a time. Noose: the target takes extra damage from every hit.
