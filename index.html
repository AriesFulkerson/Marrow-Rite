Marrow Rite

Every hundred years a dead king holds a tournament to choose who sits beside his throne. The losers become the throne. Build a team of three, tag between them, and play your cards like combos.

“Died nine centuries ago and refused the grave.”

Vorhul, the Unburied, rules from a throne made of the Rite's losers. Monks who burned their gods' houses, surgeons struck from the guild, giants buried in gate foundations: every century, the worst of the world is dragged into his arena to fight for a seat at his side.

Marrow Rite is dark fantasy painted in the tradition of Caravaggio and Goya: one hard light, deep shadow, dread over gore.

How it plays

Fast, readable turns that feel like a fighting game: build pressure, chain hits, and tag in the right fighter at the right moment.

Teams of three

Bring three fighters and tag between them. The fighter in front takes the hits; the ones behind wait for their moment.

Energy & Meter

Spend Energy to play cards each turn. Build Meter to power EX versions of your Specials for bigger effects.

Status that stacks

Burn, Bleed, Venom, Noose and Guard all interact. Stack them, then cash them in with a finisher.

Packs & collection

Open packs, build a collection, and craft your own deck from fighter and neutral cards.
