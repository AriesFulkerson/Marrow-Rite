The Rite

A century ends.
The Rite begins.

Vorhul died nine centuries ago and refused the grave. He still sits in the deepest room of his citadel, on a throne built from everyone who lost to him.

Once every hundred years the gates open and the Rite begins. Fighters come down into the pit from monasteries, swamps, guild halls and gallows. One will be chosen to sit beside the throne. The rest will be built into it.

Nine Rites have been held. The throne has grown with every one.

The fighters

Eight came down.

Every fighter plays like nobody else: a resource of their own, a passive written under their HP bar, and ten cards that only they can play.

The cards

Every card is a move.

Strikes chain Light, then Medium, then Heavy, and each hit gets +1 damage for every hit before it. Three plain punches thrown in the right order:

All art is oil-painted in the style of the set: one hard candle-light, deep shadow, bone and rust with one colour from the fighter. 53 of the 114 cards have finished paintings so far.

How it plays

Three in the pit. One at a time.

You bring a team of three. One fights while two wait on the bench. Knock out all three of theirs.

Your turn

Draw, pay, strike

Draw 2 cards. Energy refills and grows each turn, from 1 up to 8. Play as many cards as you can pay for.

Combos

Chains

Light, Medium, Heavy. Every hit adds +1 damage to the next. Drop to a lower step and the chain restarts. 3- and 5-hit chains earn Meter.

Defence

Guard and wounds

Guard soaks hits until your next turn. Burn, Bleed and Venom tick at the start of a turn and go straight through it.

Meter · max 5

EX and Finishers

Spend Meter on EX versions of cards, on Finishers that close a 2-hit chain, or on a Breaker that snaps the enemy's next 3-hit chain.

Tag team

Swap mid-combo

Once a turn, tag a benched fighter in for 1 Energy. The chain stays alive. Benched fighters slowly heal.

The end

The Last Rite

When each side has one fighter left, both heal to full. Hold 3 Meter with the enemy at 6 HP or less, and a Last Rite ends the match.

For studios

Built as a fighting game first.

Marrow Rite is an original dark-fantasy IP: a tournament, a roster, a boss and a world, designed from the start around fighting-game ideas. The card game is a working prototype of that design. Every system already speaks the language of a fighting game, so it translates directly to a real-time fighter.

The tournament gives every fight a stake and every fighter a reason to be in the pit. The bone citadel and its undying king give the roster one look and one enemy. The hundred-year cycle gives the setting room for sequels, new rosters and returning champions.

What exists today

  • A complete, playable card game with a tutorial, intro slides and full rules, against an AI opponent.
  • An online 1v1 build, plus a server that runs on a home PC for private matches.
  • Tuned with 100k balance-based simulations: AI-vs-AI matches with random teams and every starter deck.
  • Nine fighters with written bios, looks, palettes and passives; four starter decks.
  • A style bible and per-fighter art prompts, so new art stays consistent with the set.

Art direction

Painterly oil with heavy chiaroscuro: one hard warm light, near-black shadow, a bone, ash and rust palette with a single accent colour per fighter. Dread is implied, never shown. Wounds live in shadow, not close-ups.
